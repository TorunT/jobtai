Resources

Job search guides & tips

How to write a resume that gets interviews in 2025

A strong resume in 2025 must pass ATS filters before a human ever reads it. Use the exact job title from the listing, include measurable achievements (not just duties), keep it to one page for under 10 years of experience, and use clean formatting with no tables or columns. JobTAi handles all of this automatically for each job you apply to.

What recruiters look for in a resume — in under 10 seconds

Studies show recruiters spend an average of 7 seconds on an initial resume scan. They're looking for: job title match, years of experience, recognizable company names, and 2–3 standout metrics. Your resume must answer "can this person do the job?" immediately. Tailor the top third of your resume to every role.

The #1 reason your resume gets rejected before a human sees it

Over 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS software before reaching a recruiter. The most common reasons: missing keywords from the job description, using tables or graphics that ATS can't parse, saving as PDF when the job requires Word, and using headers like "Work History" instead of "Professional Experience." JobTAi's ATS optimizer prevents all of these.

How to write a cover letter that actually gets read

The best cover letters are 3 paragraphs: (1) a specific hook about why you want THIS company — not a generic opener, (2) two concrete achievements that directly match what they're hiring for, (3) a clear, confident ask for an interview. Never start with "I am writing to apply for…" — hiring managers have read that sentence a million times.

Best job search sites in the US — 2025

The most effective US job boards: LinkedIn Jobs (best for networking + applying), Indeed (highest volume, all industries), Glassdoor (salary transparency + reviews), Greenhouse / Lever (tech & startup roles), Workday (enterprise companies). Apply on the company's direct website when possible — it often bypasses aggregator filters.

Data Analyst & BI jobs in the US — what employers want in 2025

The most in-demand skills for Data Analyst roles: SQL (required everywhere), Python or R, Tableau or Power BI, cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure), and communication skills. Salary range: $85k–$165k depending on seniority and location. Remote roles now make up over 40% of data analytics postings. Highlight measurable impact — not just tools used.
